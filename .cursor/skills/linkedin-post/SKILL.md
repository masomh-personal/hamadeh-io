# LinkedIn Post

## Rules

- **No links or site promotion.** Do not include URLs to `hamadeh.io`, links to blog posts, or lines like "I wrote a post about this" or "read more on my blog" unless the user explicitly asks to include a link. Every post must stand completely on its own.
- **Match the site voice.** Follow `.cursor/rules/blog-writing-consistency.mdc`: direct, practical, personable, and grounded in real engineering tradeoffs. Use contractions and varied sentence rhythm.
- **No em dashes (`—`).** Use periods, commas, parentheses, or shorter sentences instead.
- **Avoid LinkedIn clichés and AI filler.** No broetry (one-sentence-per-line drama), no "Agree?", no "Here's why that matters:", no hype.
- **Never include AI or Cursor attribution.**

## Structure

1. **Hook (1-2 short lines):** Lead with the real tension or takeaway, not a generic intro.
2. **Concrete example or core insight:** Ground the idea in a realistic engineering scenario or tradeoff.
3. **Scannable breakdown (short bullets or tight paragraphs):** Keep it easy to read on mobile.
4. **Closing thought or genuine question:** End with something worth discussing.
5. **Hashtags:** 3 to 5 relevant, specific tags at the bottom.
